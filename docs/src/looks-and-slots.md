# Looks, Saved Slots & Settings Panel

## 12 Ready-Made Looks
Apply curated trail + halo + color combinations in a single click:
`Classic`, `Neon`, `Ember`, `Bubbles`, `Echo`, `Beads`, `Aurora`, `Drift`, `Presenter`, `Crosshair`, `Target`, `Minimal`.

## 16 Ready-Made Cursors
Apply full forged cursor styles in one click:
`Winscroller`, `Ink`, `Paper`, `Accent`, `Neon`, `Glass`, `Candy`, `Gilded`, `Marble`, `Nebula`, `Stitched`, `Dot`, `Sharp`, `Glitch`, `Terrazzo`, `Hologram`.

## 12 Saved Places
Each slot stores your entire configuration snapshot (effects, cursor design, panel theme, behavior):
- **Left-click empty**: Save current settings.
- **Left-click occupied (`*`)**: Load saved settings.
- **Right-click**: Overwrite slot.
- **Middle-click**: Clear slot.

## Undo & Clipboard Sharing
Use **Undo** to revert the last Look/Cursor/Slot change, **Copy Look JSON** to share a look as text, and **Paste Look JSON** to apply someone else's look.
